How rotation guidance is generated

Transparent conditions, not fabricated forecasts

The suggestions are rule-based educational examples. They consider crop family, known field conditions, survey drainage, and seasonal cover benefits. No unverified “98% fit,” predicted nitrogen credit, erosion reduction, yield, or profit is calculated. A farmer should confirm local planting calendars, soil tests, diseases, equipment, and markets before acting.

🔗 Pair your ESP32

Generate a private upload token for your Auth0 account. Keep it out of source control.

Private device
Pairing is required once per ESP32. Use the Arduino firmware in the downloadable project.

The device upload token controls hardware uploads. Do not share it with another account; use the separate viewing token below.

👥 Share ESP32 readings

Let another verified Auth0 account view your readings without permission to upload or change them.

View only
Generate a viewing token after your ESP32 has sent its first reading. Give this token to the other account.

Checking your shared device connection…

Viewing tokens are read-only, expire after 180 days, and can be revoked by the device owner. They do not grant control of the ESP32.
